Common questions

How accurate are the AI estimates?

Photo and description estimates are approximate. Check the food names and portions on the review screen before saving. Foods from search and barcodes use USDA and Open Food Facts data.

How do I turn off AI estimates?

Settings → AI estimates → turn off "Allow AI estimates".

How do I connect or disconnect Apple Health?

Use the Apple Health toggle in Settings. Change individual permissions in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Morsel.

How do I delete my data?

Your log is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. Anything already saved to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.
