Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026 (on-device estimates)

Morsel has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your food log, profile and targets are stored only on your iPhone.

What leaves your device

  • AI estimates on your iPhone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iOS 27 and later), photo and description estimates run on the device with Apple's on-device model. The photo and description never leave your iPhone; only the food names it finds are looked up in USDA FoodData Central.
  • AI estimates on other iPhones (Google Gemini). Only on iPhones that can't run on-device estimates, and only after you allow it, the meal photo you take or pick, or the meal you type, is sent to Google's Gemini API to estimate its nutrition. Nothing else is sent with it. Google handles this data under the Gemini API terms and may use it to improve its services. You can turn AI estimates off in Settings at any time.
  • Food search. Search words are sent to USDA FoodData Central, and scanned barcodes are sent to Open Food Facts, to look up nutrition data.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health, Morsel saves the calories, protein, carbs and fat of foods you log, and reads your weight, active energy and steps to show trends and adjust your target. Health data stays on your device and in Apple Health. It is never sent to Google or anyone else, and never used for advertising.

Photos and camera

Photos are used only to estimate a meal and to show a small thumbnail in your log. Morsel doesn't read the rest of your library.

Notifications

Meal reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No data is sent to send them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything Morsel stored. Remove anything saved to Apple Health in the Health app.

Children

Morsel isn't directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Targets and estimates in Morsel are general information, not medical advice.
